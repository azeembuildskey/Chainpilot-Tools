02 — Reference

Quick reference for export teams

The essentials worth keeping open on a busy documentation day.

Incoterms 2020

11 rules
Any mode of transport
EXW
Ex Works — buyer collects at seller's premises; seller does the least.
FCA
Free Carrier — seller hands goods to the carrier named by the buyer.
CPT
Carriage Paid To — seller pays carriage to destination; risk passes at first carrier.
CIP
Carriage & Insurance Paid To — CPT plus seller buys all-risk insurance.
DAP
Delivered At Place — seller delivers, ready for unloading, at the named place.
DPU
Delivered At Place Unloaded — seller delivers and unloads at the named place.
DDP
Delivered Duty Paid — seller bears everything, including import duties.
Sea & inland waterway only
FAS
Free Alongside Ship — seller delivers alongside the vessel at the port.
FOB
Free On Board — risk passes once goods are on board the vessel.
CFR
Cost & Freight — seller pays freight to destination port; risk passes on board.
CIF
Cost, Insurance & Freight — CFR plus marine insurance.

General guidance, not advice for a specific shipment — always confirm terms against your contract and LC.

Core export documents

checklist
  • Commercial Invoice — parties, goods, value, Incoterms, currency.
  • Packing List — packages, weights, dimensions, marks.
  • Bill of Lading / AWB — the transport contract & title document.
  • Certificate of Origin — where the goods were produced.
  • Letter of Credit docs — when payment is on LC terms (UCP 600).
  • Insurance Certificate — required under CIF / CIP.
  • Shipping Bill / Export Declaration — customs filing.
  • Inspection / Phytosanitary cert — product & country dependent.

Requirements vary by country, commodity and buyer — treat this as a starting checklist.
